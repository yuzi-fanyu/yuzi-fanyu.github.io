My story

Yuzi, a visual communication designer based in Beijing. She graduated in 2022 with a Bachelor's degree from Xiamen University, and is currently pursuing a Master's degree at Beijing Institute of Technology. Her work focuses on cultural identity, social issues, and narrative design, blending traditional culture with contemporary visual expression. She has received over 20 national and international design awards, and her projects have been showcased in major exhibitions and competitions. Through her design, she seeks to convey cultural warmth and social reflection, while continuously expanding the boundaries of cross-disciplinary and cross-cultural creativity.
