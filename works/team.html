Yu Chenglong Integrity-Themed Calendar Design2023

The illustrations were designed by my collaborator, Zhang Jiaxin, while I was responsible for the poster layout design.

This calendar is inspired by the family teachings of Yu Chenglong and his legacy of integrity.
Each month features a traditional virtue shown through guochao-style illustrations.
The design blends cultural storytelling with elements of Yu Chenglong's former residence.

Sincere thanks to Associate Professor Dong Hongyu for her invaluable guidance on the project.
