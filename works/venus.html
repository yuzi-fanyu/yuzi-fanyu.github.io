Fenjiu Rolling Stamp


Concept

This project reimagines traditional Chinese seal art in a contemporary and interactive format. Centered around the idea of cultural participation, the DIY rolling stamp allows users to create their own printed patterns, turning passive packaging into a meaningful hands-on experience.

Design Inspiration

Drawing from Chinese idioms and auspicious symbols like "步步高升" (Step by Step Rise), the stamp motifs incorporate poetic phrases and classical patterns. Each design blends visual elegance with heartfelt blessings, embodying the emotional connection behind gift-giving.

Materials & Craftsmanship

Crafted from natural materials such as wood, ceramic, and cotton, the gift set emphasizes tactile quality and traditional craftsmanship. The use of a rolling mechanism adds a playful yet nostalgic touch, echoing ancient printing methods while appealing to modern aesthetics.

Exhibition & Media

The project was presented as a multi-sensory installation featuring mirror-foil artist books and square folding zines, resin-cast objects that freeze digital moments in physical form, looping short videos designed around the 6-second attention span, large-format holographic posters that create an immersive visual impact, and interactive elements that blur the line between virtual perception and physical space.

User Interaction

Unlike static packaging, this gift set invites the recipient to actively participate in the cultural ritual of "sealing" — rolling the stamp to imprint personal messages. This process enhances emotional engagement and transforms a liquor gift into a customizable keepsake.

Cultural Significance

This project bridges heritage and innovation by embedding cultural literacy into commercial packaging. It not only reflects traditional values and craftsmanship but also aligns with modern consumers' pursuit of personalization and cultural identity.
