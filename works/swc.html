Flora Deity

"Flora Deity" is an IP series designed for the Garden of World's Flowers, brought to life at Caoqiao Subway Station in Beijing.

"The Twelve Flora Deities" — Plum, Apricot, Peach, Peony, Pomegranate, Lotus, Gardenia, Osmanthus, Chrysanthemum, Hibiscus, Camellia, and Narcissus. Twelve flower deities, each representing a bloom of the twelve months, bringing beauty to every season.

King Boat Festival

A folk song from coastal Minnan and Taiwan depicts the ancient ritual of sending off the King Boat.
It is brought to life through dynamic illustrations that capture this unique tradition.

This character trio reimagines the King Boat Festival with bold, stylized figures rooted in coastal folk belief.
From devoted fishers to the mighty Chiwangye, each reflects a unique role in this ritual of protection and renewal.
